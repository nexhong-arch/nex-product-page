Project Overview

Integrated Metal-Wood Fabrication for a Live Airport Environment

Delivering custom fabrication in a live international airport presented unique challenges — tight scheduling windows, stringent quality standards, and zero tolerance for errors.

Our scope covered check-in counters with metal framing and wood paneling, decorative farm boxes, stepped seating units, and raised platform systems. Every component was fabricated in our Foshan factory, with final assembly and installation carried out by our professional on-site team.

The combination of metal structural integrity and warm wood finishes delivered a premium passenger experience while meeting Hong Kong airport's rigorous durability requirements.
