Project Overview

Branded Interior Expression for a Premium Airport Facility

The Airport Administrative Building required feature walls and signage that reflect the high standards of a world-class international airport. Our scope covered the complete design, fabrication, and installation of branded feature walls and illuminated signage elements.

The feature wall combines aluminum framing with stainless steel decorative elements, creating a modern, professional aesthetic appropriate for an airport environment. Custom illuminated signage provides clear wayfinding while reinforcing the facility's brand identity.

All elements were fabricated to airport-grade specifications, with particular attention to fire safety compliance, accessibility standards, and long-term durability in a high-traffic public building.
