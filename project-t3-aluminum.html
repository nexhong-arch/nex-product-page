Project Overview

Precision Aluminum Fabrication for a World-Class Terminal

Hong Kong International Airport Terminal 3 represents the highest standards in modern airport architecture. Our scope covered the complete fabrication and installation of custom aluminum panel systems throughout the terminal.

Every panel was precision-cut using laser technology, formed with CNC bending machines, and finished with fluorocarbon (PVDF) coating for long-term durability against Hong Kong's coastal climate.

The stainless steel edge trim was fabricated in-house, ensuring clean transitions and a premium finish that meets airport-grade quality specifications.

Specifications

  • 3mm fluorocarbon-coated (PVDF) aluminum sheets
  • Precision laser cutting — zero tolerance gaps
  • CNC bending for complex panel geometry
  • Stainless steel edge trim
  • Weather-resistant coating system
  • Full in-house quality inspection
  • Professional on-site installation
  • Airport compliance standards met
